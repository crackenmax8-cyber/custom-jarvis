## Description (4000 max)

```
PepTalk is a harm-reduction reference and personal tracker for people who are
already using — or seriously considering — anabolic steroids, SARMs, peptides
and the ancillary drugs that surround them.

It does not tell you to use anything. It says plainly, throughout, that not
using is the safest option, that nothing here is medical advice, and that
bloodwork and a doctor beat guesswork every time. What it does is make the
harm that is happening visible and manageable.

WHAT TO TAKE, AND WHY
• 53 compounds across nine classes: injectable and oral steroids, SARMs,
  ancillaries and PCT drugs, fat-loss and thyroid agents, growth-hormone
  peptides, GLP-1s and recovery peptides.
• For each one: what it actually does, its real risks, the nutrients it
  depletes, the supportive supplements people use and why, the bloodwork to
  run, and the warnings that matter.
• Every compound carries a severity rating shown as a written label, never
  colour alone.

BUILD YOUR STACK, GET THE SUPPORT AUTOMATICALLY
• Pick what you are running. PepTalk assembles the support plan itself:
  the vitamins and nutrients your choices call for, de-duplicated, with the
  lab panels to book and the combined-risk flags that only appear when
  compounds interact — like two oral 17-aa compounds multiplying liver load.
• A "what this stack runs low on" breakdown, per compound.

TRACK WHAT IS ACTUALLY HAPPENING
• Log injections, blood pressure, weight, bloodwork panels and side effects.
• Charts with reference ranges shaded and out-of-range readings flagged.
• Blood results are read cycle-aware: markers that run high on cycle by design
  read as expected, while genuinely dangerous drift — a haematocrit of 54%,
  hypertensive-crisis blood pressure, crushed HDL, rising liver enzymes —
  flags loudly. The signal stays meaningful.
• An injection-site map that tracks rotation and suggests the most rested site.
• A printable one-page summary to hand a clinician.

WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG
• 20 side effects, each answered in three honest tiers: what is free (lower the
  dose, cardio, sleep, hydrate — usually the real fix), what is over the
  counter, and what is an actual prescription drug, with the risks of each
  spelled out.
• Emergency warning signs by urgency, one tap from anywhere — the symptoms
  that mean stop and get help now.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• Everything stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no
  network requests at all. Your data never leaves the phone.
• Export or delete everything whenever you want.

IMPORTANT
PepTalk is educational harm-reduction information, not medical advice, not a
diagnosis, and not an endorsement to use anything. Doses referenced are
commonly-reported ranges, not prescriptions. Reference ranges are context-aware
but are not a clinical assessment. Anabolic steroids and many compounds
described are controlled substances in many countries; possession and supply
may be illegal where you live. Always work with a qualified doctor, get
baseline bloodwork before anything, and treat "don't" as a legitimate answer.
```

## Notes for App Review (this is the important one)

Paste this into *App Review Information → Notes*. A borderline app is judged in
large part on whether the reviewer understands the intent within thirty seconds.

```
PepTalk is a harm-reduction and health-tracking app. It is aimed at adults who
are already using anabolic steroids, SARMs or peptides, and its purpose is to
reduce the medical harm of that use — the same public-health model as needle
exchange or drug-checking services.

Please note specifically:

1. The app does not encourage, promote or glamorise use, and does not tell
   anyone to take anything. A permanent banner on every screen reads "Harm
   reduction, not medical advice" and states that not using is always the
   safest option. That framing is repeated throughout the content.

2. The app sells nothing, sources nothing and links to no vendor, marketplace
   or supplier. There is no commerce of any kind and no way to obtain any
   substance through it.

3. It provides no dosing protocols or instructions for use. Where quantities
   appear they are commonly-reported ranges given as context for risk, and are
   labelled as not prescriptions.

4. The core function is health monitoring: logging blood pressure, weight and
   laboratory results, charting them against reference ranges, flagging
   dangerous values (for example a haematocrit of 54%), listing the warning
   signs that require emergency care, and producing a printable summary to
   give a doctor. It repeatedly directs the user to seek medical care.

5. It is entirely offline. No accounts, no analytics, no network requests, no
   third-party SDKs. All data stays in local storage on the device and can be
   exported or deleted by the user.

6. Rated 17+ with Medical/Treatment Information and Drug References both set to
   Frequent/Intense.

No demo account is needed — all features are available immediately with no
sign-in. To see the tracking features populated, open "My protocol", add any
compound with a start date, and the Today, Trends and Bloodwork views become
active.
```
