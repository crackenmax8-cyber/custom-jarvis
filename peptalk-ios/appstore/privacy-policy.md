# PepTalk — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 9 August 2026**

## The short version

PepTalk collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking, and the app makes no network requests. Everything you enter stays on
your device.

## What data the app stores

PepTalk stores the following **locally on your device only**, using your
browser/WebView local storage:

- Your saved protocol (compounds, doses, esters, start date, cycle length, and
  the sex setting used to pick reference ranges).
- Your log entries: injections and sites, blood pressure, heart rate, weight,
  bloodwork results, side effects and notes.
- Your selected stack and interface preferences (such as light or dark theme).

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us, because
there is no server to transmit it to.

## What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, share or process any personal
data. We have no ability to see your data.

## Network access

The App Store build of PepTalk makes **no network requests of any kind**. It
contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers and
no third-party services. It functions fully with the device in aeroplane mode.

## Health data

PepTalk does **not** read from or write to Apple Health (HealthKit). The health
information you type in is stored only in the app's own local storage.

## Your control over your data

- **Export** — "Data & backup" produces a JSON file containing everything you
  have entered, which you control entirely.
- **Delete** — "Data & backup" also clears all stored data permanently.
- **Uninstalling** the app removes all of its local data from your device.

Because we never receive your data, requests to access, correct or delete data
held by us are not applicable — there is nothing held by us.

## Children

PepTalk is rated 17+ and is not directed at children. It is not intended for
anyone under 18.

## Medical disclaimer

PepTalk provides educational harm-reduction information. It is not medical
advice, not a diagnosis, and not a recommendation to use any substance. Always
consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address
with an updated date. Because the app collects nothing, any change would be to
clarify wording rather than to expand data use.
